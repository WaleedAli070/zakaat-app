HEADLINE VERDICTS ON THE TWO CONTRADICTIONS
- Hawl: Gemini is WRONG. Maliki = continuous (like Shafi'i/Hanbali), not start/end. Qaradawi vol.1 p.135-136; al-Nawawi "our view and the view of Malik, Ahmad and the majority ... nisab be present throughout the year" (islamqa.info/99381). Only exception: for TRADE GOODS Qaradawi (p.168) reports Malik + al-Umm text as "nisab need only exist at year-END" — probably the origin of Gemini's confusion.
- Debts: Gemini is RIGHT that classical Maliki AND Hanbali both deduct debts; the draft's "Conditional: only if no fixed assets (house/car)" is a garbled version of a real rule (offset against SURPLUS non-zakatable assets beyond basic needs — a residence/family car never offsets). That offset rule is attested for Maliki (islamweb 324727, 298531) and for Hanbali via Ibn Qudamah (islamweb 426541). Shafi'i (new view) = no deduction. Contemporary Saudi/Hanbali-leaning islamqa.info (Ibn Baz/Ibn 'Uthaymin) = no deduction except debts currently demanded (islamqa.info/22426, /120371) — label as "Ibn 'Uthaymin / islamqa.info", not "Hanbali madhhab".
- Annexation of new wealth (missing from draft): Hanafi annex; Shafi'i own hawl; Hanbali own hawl (Ibn Qudamah); Maliki own hawl for money but annex for livestock. Growth/profit of existing capital annexed by consensus. Qaradawi vol.1 p.72-73, p.258; islamqa.info/93414.

## 1. NISAB
Grams:
- 85 g gold / 595 g silver: islamqa.info (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/2795 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/370380 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/64), Zakat Foundation of America (https://www.zakat.org/what-is-ni-ab-in-islam), Zaytuna calculator (https://zakatcalculator.zaytuna.edu/), AMJA (https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/79050/how-much-is-the-nisab-for-zakat-now ; https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/80593/zakat-on-silver-and-gold), NZF Australia (https://www.nzf.org.au/blog/what-is-nisab/), NZF Canada (https://www.nzfcanada.com/zakat-faq/what-is-nisab), Joe Bradford (https://joebradford.net/nisab-for-zakat-on-gold-and-silver/), Qaradawi vol.1 p.130 ("200 x 2.975 = 595 grams ... 20 x 4.25 = 85 grams"). Bradford's newsletter attributes 85/595 to "Dar al-Ifta of Al-Azhar, Jordan, Malaysia, Morocco, KSA, and AAOIFI" and 87.48/612.36 to the Hanafi school via Mufti Muhammad Shafi (https://joebradford.net/newsletter/nisab-what-it-is-and-how-to-calculate-it/).
- 87.48 g gold / 612.36 g silver (7.5 / 52.5 tola; variants 87.479/87.47 and 612.35): Askimam (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/1346/), Darul Ifta Deoband [snippet] (https://darulifta-deoband.com/home/en/zakat-charity/39079), Qibla/Ilyas Patel (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/qibla-hanafi/34746/the-minimum-zakatable-amount-nisab/), Darul Ifta Birmingham (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/171143/combining-the-value-of-gold-and-silver-for-zakat/), NZF UK ("87.48 grams of gold and 612.36 grams of silver (85 grams ... is another opinion)" https://nzf.org.uk/nisab/), Islamic Relief US (https://www.islamic-relief.org/zakat/nisab/), SeekersGuidance calculator (https://www.seekersguidance.org/zakatcalculator/). Outlier 84 g / 592.9 g: SeekersGuidance Shafi'i answer (https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/should-the-minimum-zakatable-amount-be-measured-by-the-gold-or-silver-standards/).
Combining gold + silver (+ cash) to reach nisab:
- Hanafi: YES by value — Birmingham (URL above), Qibla 42568 (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/qibla-hanafi/42568/zakat-on-gold-and-silver-5/), cash+gold TheMufti (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/themufti/153326/combining-gold-and-cash-for-zakaat-calculation-purposes/). High.
- Maliki: YES — al-Mughni quoted on islamqa.info: "al-Hasan, Qatadah, al-Awza'i, al-Thawri, Malik, Abu Hanifah and all of ashab al-ra'y said that one of them may be added to the other" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/144734); islamweb calls combining the majority view (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/81848/adding-gold-and-silver-together-for-zakah). High.
- Hanbali: TWO NARRATIONS from Ahmad — Ibn Qudamah al-Kafi: "Gold cannot be added to silver to complete the nisab because they are two different types", and "it was narrated from Ahmad ... that he added them together"; Ibn 'Uthaymin/islamqa.info prefers No "except when they are used for exchange" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/144734); islamweb groups Hanbalis with the combining majority (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/81848/adding-gold-and-silver-together-for-zakah). The madhhab's relied view could not be established from fetched sources. Medium — surface as ikhtilaf.
- Shafi'i: NO — al-Nawawi: "Gold cannot be added to silver or vice versa ... There is no difference of scholarly opinion in our madhhab" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/144734 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/201807). High.
- Cash with metals: combined in all schools (cash is valued against the metal nisab) — islamqa.info 201807 quoting Standing Committee (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/201807); Hanafi sources above. High.
Silver vs gold preference:
- Silver (lower, "better for the poor / safer"): NZF UK "using silver is safer and more beneficial for the recipients" (https://nzf.org.uk/nisab/); NZF Canada (https://www.nzfcanada.com/zakat-faq/what-is-nisab); Islamic Relief (https://www.islamic-relief.org/zakat/nisab/); islamqa.info rule = "whichever of the two works out better for the poor" = currently silver (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/2795 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/370380 citing MWL Fiqh Council & Permanent Committee); Joe Bradford personal position silver (https://joebradford.net/nisab-for-zakat-on-gold-and-silver/); SeekersGuidance calc default silver; Deobandi (Askimam 1346; Qibla 34746; Deoband 41452 [snippet]: gold nisab only if one owns gold alone). Qaradawi p.131 notes silver is "commonly used in countries like Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the gulf states, India, and Pakistan".
- Gold: Zakat Foundation of America ("gold has proved ... a more just wealth measure" https://www.zakat.org/nisab-and-zakat-calculation-in-a-nutshell); Qaradawi p.131 ("much better to determine the nisab on money today at the equivalent of ... 85 grams of gold, because doing it otherwise would inflict great injustice on zakah payers"); Shaykh Taha Karaan (Shafi'i) via SeekersGuidance (URL above). AMJA computes from 85 g gold without stating a preference (79050). Zaytuna offers both, no recommendation. FCNA: no statement found.
- "Safer" argument: silver = lower threshold → more payers → more for the poor (NZF UK, islamqa.info 64 "most conservative"). Counter: silver makes the poor pay (Karaan). Both documented; this is an ikhtilaf to surface, with silver as the majority institutional default.

## 2. HAWL
Start/end vs continuous (money, gold, silver):
- Hanafi: nisab at start AND end; dips ignored; hawl resets only if wealth falls to zero/negative (NZF UK https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/is-it-necessary-to-own-zakatable-assets-above-the-nisab-threshold-for-the-entire-year/) or to "only enough for living expenses" (SeekersGuidance https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/do-i-pay-zakat-only-on-money-ive-had-for-over-a-year/). Qaradawi p.135. High.
- Maliki: continuous — Qaradawi p.136 ("the other three schools ... require that nisab stay in ownership during the whole year"); al-Nawawi attributes continuity to "Malik, Ahmad and the majority" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/99381); Zakat Foundation of America groups Maliki with Shafi'i/Hanbali (https://www.zakat.org/when-is-zakat-due). One Maliki softening: Malik tolerates a negligible shortfall ("one or two grains") (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/128168). Trade-goods exception: Malik/al-Umm — year-end only (Qaradawi p.168). High for cash; Medium nuance for trade goods.
- Shafi'i: continuous; dip of even "an hour" cancels (al-Nawawi, islamqa.info/128168; /99381). High.
- Hanbali: continuous — al-Buhuti: "If the (wealth) dips below the nisab partway through the year, then it is cancelled" (islamqa.info/99381); islamweb Arabic 364963 "فإن نقص فإن الحول ينقطع" (https://www.islamweb.net/ar/fatwa/364963/). High. (Claim that "some Hanbalis count only start/end" was found only on a non-authoritative site and is contradicted by al-Mughni as summarized; drop it.)
- Zero mid-year: all schools reset; new hawl starts when nisab is re-attained (islamqa.info/99381; NZF UK).
Annexation of newly acquired wealth:
- Consensus: profit/growth of existing capital joins the original hawl (Ibn Qudamah "I am aware of no dispute on this", Qaradawi p.72; islamqa.info/93414 "the profit on money is connected to the capital").
- Hanafi: independent new wealth of the same genus (salary, gift) is ADDED and paid at the existing date (Qaradawi p.73; SeekersGuidance Reasat URL above; Birmingham "regardless of when he acquired wealth during the course of the year" https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/77184/zakat-on-goods-and-merchandise-2/). High.
- Shafi'i: own hawl — "The Hawl of the new money starts from the date of its earning" (Darul Iftaa Jordan https://islamqa.org/shafii/darul-iftaa-jordan/227971/ruling-on-money-earned-during-hawl/); Qaradawi p.73, p.258. High.
- Hanbali: own hawl — "not zakatable in that year, according to Ahmad and al-Shafi'i" (Qaradawi p.73 citing al-Mughni); islamqa.info/93414 "a separate year should be counted for it". High. NOTE Joe Bradford groups Hanbali with Hanafi (https://joebradford.net/the-hawl-passing-of-one-lunar-year-for-zakat-liability/) — contradicted by Ibn Qudamah; treat as simplified.
- Maliki: money = own hawl; livestock = annexed ("Malik agrees with Abu Hanifah in the case of pastured livestock, while in money he tends to agree with Ahmad and al-Shafi'i", Qaradawi p.73; same via Ibn Hazm p.258; Bradford same). Medium-High. NOTE Zakat Foundation of America groups Maliki with Hanafi (https://www.zakat.org/when-is-zakat-due) — contradicted by Qaradawi; treat as simplified.
- Practical escape hatch used by non-Hanafi bodies: pay on everything at one date as voluntary advance payment — islamqa.info/93414 ("nothing wrong with that"), Ibn 'Uthaymin "choose a particular month ... pay zakah on all of it" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/50801). So an app may default to one date in all modes but must label the surplus as advance zakat in non-Hanafi modes.

## 3. PERSONAL JEWELRY
- Hanafi: all gold and silver zakatable whether worn or stored; >50% gold content counts as gold (Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/124146/zakat-on-gold-jewellery-2/ ; https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/126605/zakat-on-gold-of-different-carats-2/). High.
- Shafi'i: exempt if lawful, reasonable, used (even once a year), not hoarded as investment — "official position of the Shafi'i School (as well as the Maliki and Hanbali Schools)" (SeekersGuidance https://seekersguidance.org/answers/zakat-shafii-fiqh/what-constitutes-the-type-of-jewellery-wearing-that-obviates-the-need-to-pay-zakat-thereon/ ; https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/must-women-pay-zakat-on-their-gold-jewelry/). High.
- 200 mithqal (Shafi'i): VERIFIED as an illustration only. Shaykh Taha Karaan: scholars "illustrated extravagance with an anklet (khalkhal) weighing 200 mithqals (which, at 968 grams, falls just slightly short of 1kg)"; real test is "comely" vs "grotesque"; zakat then "only on that one piece" (https://islamqa.org/shafii/shafiifiqh/30157/questions-regarding-zakah-on-extravagant-jewelry/). Contrast Shaykh Abdurragmaan Khan (SeekersGuidance): if excessive by community custom "zakah is compulsory on all of it". Qaradawi p.148: Nawawi — lawful "only within the limits of non-apparent extravagance"; Ibn Hamid (Hanbali) 1,000 mithqal; Ibn Qudamah rejects any limit. Do NOT hard-code 200 mithqal as a threshold; it is an example and the mithqal weight itself varies (4.25 g Qaradawi vs 4.84 g Karaan).
- Maliki & Hanbali: exempt when worn/lawful. Qaradawi vol.1 p.147 quotes Malik: "Those who own gold and jewelry made of gold or silver which is not used for ornamental wearing, must pay zakah on it every year" (i.e., worn jewelry exempt; Maliki quirk: unworn jewelry below 40 dinars/200 dirhams also exempt; broken jewelry awaiting repair still counts as used); p.148: "Hanbalites have established the opinion that any person who holds jewelry as a means to avoid zakah is zakatable" (otherwise exempt); SeekersGuidance confirms the Maliki/Hanbali exemption. High.
- Men's gold: haram to wear in all schools (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/148476); "Scholars agreed that zakah is obligatory on gold and silver jewellery if it is a kind of adornment that it is prohibited to use, or if it is prepared for trading" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/59866/zakaah-on-jewellery-that-has-been-prepared-for-use). High — men's gold is zakatable in all four schools.
- Silver jewelry: same rule as gold per school; men's silver ring lawful (Hanafi limit 4.5 masha, https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/7884/) → exempt in non-Hanafi, zakatable in Hanafi.
- Precious stones/diamonds not for trade: no zakat, majority (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/40210/there-is-no-zakaah-on-precious-stones-unless-they-are-for-trade).
- islamqa.info (Saudi) prefers the minority-but-cautious view that ALL worn jewellery is zakatable (https://m.islamqa.info/en/answers/145770 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/19901) — this is a Hanbali-leaning body departing from the Hanbali madhhab; label it as such.

## 4. DEBTS OWED BY THE USER
Classical:
- Hanafi: debt prevents zakat except on crops/fruits (SeekersGuidance Fayez Awad https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/which-debts-affect-zakat-for-both-debtor-and-creditor/); deductible = debts "demanded and claimed by other people" incl. mahr and zakat demanded by the imam (Darul Iftaa Leicester https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/8382/zakat-and-the-subtraction-of-loans/). Qaradawi p.69 lists Abu Hanifah with the majority. High.
- Maliki: debt prevents zakat on gold/silver/trade goods only (not livestock/crops) (SeekersGuidance Fayez Awad); AND offset first against surplus non-zakatable assets: "If you have tradable items such as real estate property and so forth that reach the amount of the debt and you do not need them, then you should consider that they offset the debt and then pay the zakah on whatever remains" (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/324727/calculating-zakah-when-one-is-creditor-and-debtor ; https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/298531/zakah-on-a-debt-that-has-to-be-returned-over-nine-years). High for "deducts"; High for the offset condition.
- Hanbali: classical = debt deducted (Qaradawi p.69 lists Ahmad with the majority); Ibn Qudamah: "The debt prevents the payment of Zakaah only when its value consumes the Nisaab or the remaining amount ... is less than the Nisaab, and the debtor cannot afford repaying the debt except with the wealth liable for Zakaah or wealth with which he cannot dispense" (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/426541/deducting-the-value-of-debt-from-the-nisaab-when-paying-zakaah) — i.e., same surplus-asset offset. Medium-High. Contemporary islamqa.info/Ibn 'Uthaymin: no deduction (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/22426).
- Shafi'i: no deduction — "being in debt does not exempt one from paying zakat, even if one's debts outweigh all of one's assets" (Hamza Karamali https://islamqa.org/shafii/qibla-shafii/33932/zakat-debts-zakat-and-sadaqa/); Qaradawi p.69 ("Shafi'i in his new opinion"). High.
- Fiqh Encyclopedia principle (via islamweb 324727/426541): whoever deducts must first set surplus non-zakatable assets beyond basic needs against the debt. Attested for Maliki and Hanbali; contemporary Hanafi fatwa bodies (NZF, Askimam, SeekersGuidance) deduct without stating such a test.
Immediate vs long-term — the "12-month rule": issued by NZF UK (deduct "Up to 12 lunar months of instalments of longer-term debts", non-interest portion, and only "if paying Zakat is likely to stop you being able to afford the repayments" https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/payment-of-zakat-deductible-liabilities/ ; https://nzf.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025_NZF_General-Zakat-Guide.pdf), NZF Canada (next 12 months of principal on the primary residence mortgage; rental-property mortgages not deductible https://www.nzfcanada.com/zakat-faq/how-do-i-pay-zakat-if-i-have-multiple-mortgages), Askimam ("Long term loans do not prevent the obligation of Zakat whereas short term loans [within one year] prevent" https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/124321/zakaat-on-debt/ ; yearly instalments only https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/102522/do-i-have-to-pay-zakaat-while-i-have-debts-to-pay/), Darul Ifta Birmingham (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/20383/), SeekersGuidance Tabraze Azam ("up to a year's worth of repayments, and not the entire debt" https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/calculating-zakat-with-long-term-debts/), Joe Bradford 2025 guide (https://joebradford.substack.com/p/how-to-calculate-your-zakat). NOT AMJA, NOT Fiqh Council NA (no debt resolution found). Mufti Taqi Usmani attribution: only a footnote citation "Fatawa Uthmani 4/70-71" on Askimam; not verified from his own text; his verified view is personal loans deductible vs business loans only if used for zakatable goods (Leicester 8382).
- Single-due-instalment camp: AMJA/Al-Qudah "The only amount deductible ... is the payment which is due" (https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/21598/how-to-pay-zakat-while-still-paying-off-a-mortgage ; https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/85247/zakat-question); Zakat Foundation of America (one-year reading "a weak interpretation and cautioned against" https://www.zakat.org/can-home-mortgages-be-deducted-from-zakat-as-debt ; student loans "No" https://www.zakat.org/can-student-loans-be-deducted-from-zakat-as-debt); Joe Bradford debt article (contradicts his own guide) https://joebradford.substack.com/p/deducting-debt-from-your-zakat-calculation ; Darul Iftaa Leicester 8395 (only short-term) https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/8395/zakat-on-long-and-short-term-loans/ ; SeekersGuidance Waseem Hussain https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/zakat-long-term-debts/.
- No-deduction camp: NZF Australia "You are not allowed to deduct your home mortgage or study (HECS) debts" (https://www.nzf.org.au/blog/zakat-guide-for-individuals-families/); islamqa.info 120371 ("Neither has any impact on zakaah").
- Full-deduction outlier: FatwaCentre (Mangera/Patel) if intending to pay off (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/fatwacentre/179105/zakat-on-mortgage-payments/). Qaradawi p.70 selects "all debts, immediate and deferred are deductible".
- Specific items: mahr deductible (deferred and immediate) per SeekersGuidance Hanafi (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/seekersguidance-hanafi/168053/is-deferred-mahr-deducted-from-ones-assets-when-calculating-zakat/) and NZF UK guide; credit-card balance (Bradford full balance; NZF "within 12 months"); arrears/overdue bills deductible, next month's rent not (NZF UK/CA); taxes "formally assessed and payable" (Bradford); interest never deductible (NZF UK https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/can-i-deduct-the-monthly-payment-i-make-in-an-interest-only-mortgage/); student loan deductible only if currently repaying, 12 months of capital (NZF UK https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/i-am-repaying-my-student-loan-right-now-what-can-i-deduct-from-my-zakat-calculation/) vs never (NZF AU, zakat.org).

## 5. DEBTS OWED TO THE USER (receivables)
- Hanafi three-tier (by origin — classical scheme): qawi (loans of cash, price of trade goods) → zakat every year; may defer payment until receipt, then pay for all past years; Askimam adds payment becomes due once 1/5 of nisab is received (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/102373/zakat-on-strong-debts/ ; Birmingham https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa-birmingham/87011/zakah-on-debts/ ; MuftiOnline https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/46427/do-you-have-to-pay-zakat-on-owed-debts/). mutawassit (price of non-trade personal assets sold) and da'if (mahr, inheritance, unpaid salary, service fees): no zakat for past years; hawl starts from receipt (Birmingham 87011; MuftiOnline 46427). NOTE: the premise "mutawassit = pay for past years upon receipt" was NOT confirmed — Birmingham and Makada treat it like da'if. A second Hanafi scheme classifies by likelihood of repayment (Mathabah https://islamqa.org/hanafi/mathabah/270079/the-three-types-of-dept-and-the-implications-of-zakah/). Denied/irrecoverable debt: no zakat unless recovered (Council of Ulama EC https://islamqa.org/hanafi/councilofulama/51020/bad-debts-and-zakaat/).
- Shafi'i: good debt (solvent, acknowledging debtor) zakatable annually like a deposit; bad debt: on collection, "for the years that passed" (SeekersGuidance Ba-Dhib https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-zakat-obligatory-on-debts-that-are-not-expected-to-be-repaid/). High.
- Hanbali: annually on good debt, payable on receipt for all years; bad debt: Ahmad second report = all past years on receipt (islamqa.info/125854). Contemporary Ibn 'Uthaymin/islamqa.info: bad debt → "pay zakah once, for the year in which he took possession" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/125854/scholarly-views-concerning-zakah-on-debts-and-the-most-correct-view). High that this is the preferred view there.
- Maliki: merchant's trade receivables annually; other loans once (one year) upon receipt even after many years (islamqa.info/125854; SeekersGuidance Fayez Awad). High.
- Islamic Fiqh Council (via islamqa.info/1817): lender pays annually if debtor solvent and prompt; otherwise one year after recovery (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/1817/paying-zakaah-on-loans).
- Institutional practice: NZF UK strong/weak split in its 2025 guide; security deposit not zakatable while held (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/is-zakat-due-on-a-tenancy-deposit/) vs NZF AU pay once on return vs Bradford include if expected back (https://joebradford.substack.com/p/how-to-calculate-your-zakat). Unpaid salary: NZF "weak", Birmingham da'if, Bradford face value if acknowledged.

## 6. RETIREMENT ACCOUNTS
- (b) Annual on net withdrawable after taxes & penalty: AMJA/Al-Qudah formula "withdrawable amount – prescribed penalty – prescribed tax = zakatable amount" (https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/23284/zakat-and-the-401k-retirement-plan ; AMJA 2019 declaration s.34 https://www.amjaonline.org/declaration-articles/recommendations-of-the-16-th-annual-imams-conference-on-contemporary-financial-issues-real-estate-and-retirement-accounts); Fiqh Council NA Feb 2024 ("annual obligation due on the withdrawable amount ... after liquidating the account and paying any penalties and taxes"; employer match counts "as long as it is vested" https://fiqhcouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Zakat-on-Retirement-Accounts.pdf); Zaytuna (may subtract estimated taxes, and penalties if a withdrawal is needed to pay https://zakatcalculator.zaytuna.edu/); Zakat Foundation handbook (Al-Sawy formula WA−PP−PT https://cdn.zakat.org/wp-content/uploads/zakat-book-1.pdf); NZF Canada RRSP net of withholding tax (10/20/30% tiers — the only "30%" in any source, and it is Canada's RRSP withholding, not a fiqh figure) (https://www.nzfcanada.com/zakat-faq/how-do-i-calculate-zakat-on-my-retirement-savings-rrsp-and-tfsa).
- FCNA update 26 Feb 2026: new default Method 1 = annual zakat on the fund's zakatable-asset proportion (e.g., VOO ~24.8%; 30% S&P proxy) with NO tax/penalty deduction; Method 2 = net after tax & penalty only if intending to liquidate (example uses 40% tax + 10% penalty) (https://fiqhcouncil.org/zakah-on-retirement-funds/).
- (c) No zakat until accessible, then one year only: Joe Bradford ("not due ... until it is time to cash out without penalty ... you only pay 2.5% for one year" https://joebradford.net/pay-zakat-on-401k/); Zakat Foundation web article ("strongest opinion ... one does not have a Zakat obligation on 401(k) ... without penalty" — contradicts its own handbook https://www.zakat.org/is-zakat-owed-on-401k-plans-and-individual-retirement-accounts-ira); islamqa.info (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/252545 ; Ibn 'Uthaymin "pay zakaah for one year" on receipt https://islamqa.info/en/answers/97442); Deoband provident fund "Zakah is not wajib on PF amount right now" (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/110433/zakah-on-unwithdrawn-provident-fund/); Darul Iftaa Jordan (Shafi'i) gratuity/PF (https://islamqa.org/shafii/darul-iftaa-jordan/228940/ ; .../228649/); NZF UK defined-benefit schemes not zakatable (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/pensions/).
- (d) Annual only on voluntary/employee contributions: Deoband "if any part of the fund was deducted with your choice, you will have to pay its Zakah every year" (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/26346/); Askimam compulsory PF: no past years, voluntary PF: back-years (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/102466/ ; https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/17883/); SeekersGuidance Carr (https://seekersguidance.org/answers/zakat/how-do-i-pay-zakat-on-a-defined-contribution-scheme/).
- (a) Annual on full balance: SeekersGuidance Faraz A. Khan "advisable ... each year, based on its total value" as precaution, taxes deductible only if actually incurred (https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/zakat-on-401kretirement-plans/); NZF UK DC pensions annually but on 25% zakatable-asset proxy (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/pensions/).
- Mufti Taqi Usmani on PF/EOBI: nothing located on muftitaqiusmani.com; Deobandi bodies above carry the PF ruling. EOBI: no source found.
- Flat 30% deduction: NOT defensible as a fiqh rule — no body states it. Published examples: FCNA 40% tax + 10% penalty; Zakat Foundation 20% penalty + 25% tax; Bradford "estimated 10% penalty and estimated income tax". Make tax rate and penalty user inputs with a jurisdiction default.
- Unvested employer contributions: not zakatable (FCNA "as long as it is vested"; Bradford https://joebradford.net/zakat-on-retirement-health-and-education-accounts/).

## 7. STOCKS / FUNDS / CRYPTO
- AAOIFI SS35 (second-hand via NZF UK): trading shares = market value; long-term = zakatable assets per share "otherwise ... through estimation" (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-share-investments-determining-a-proxy-for-calculation/); FCNA cites SS35 4/2/4 and IIFA Res. 121 (3/13) (https://fiqhcouncil.org/zakah-on-stocks/). No AAOIFI fallback percentage verified.
- Proxies: NZF UK 25% ("The previous NZF recommendation of 40% is no longer applicable" — same URL; https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-stocks-and-shares/); FCNA 30% (S&P historical average; approved 27 Nov 2025, https://fiqhcouncil.org/zakah-on-stocks/); Joe Bradford 30% (CRI method, "25%–30%" mean, 30% as buffer https://joebradford.net/how-we-calculate-zakat-on-stocks/); NZF Canada 30% (https://www.nzfcanada.com/zakat-calculator); Zaytuna full current value, no proxy (https://zakatcalculator.zaytuna.edu/); Zakat Foundation full market value (handbook); AMJA/Al-Qudah: trading = market value, long-term = dividends only ("takes the rule of ... exploited assets" https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/82475/zakat-on-401k-shares), 2019 declaration = company's zakatable assets, dividends only if none; Mufti Taqi Usmani trading = full market value on anniversary (https://muftitaqiusmani.com/en/how-to-pay-zakat/); SeekersGuidance Younas: dividend shares = company's zakatable proportion (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/seekersguidance-hanafi/32606/rulings-related-to-zakat-on-shares/); islamqa.info: market value if trading; if difficult, Islamic Fiqh Council allows market value every year (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/69912). Active trading = 100% market value everywhere. The draft's "25% for all schools" is NZF UK's number only; FCNA/Bradford/NZF Canada = 30%.
- Dividends: cash on hand at anniversary (zakat.org calculator line); FCNA calls dividend/capital-gain distinction "outdated"; Bradford rejects dividends-only; AMJA 82475 dividends-only for long-term holders; "10% on dividends" recorded but not endorsed by Zakat Foundation handbook p.51.
- Index funds/ETFs/mutual funds: same as shares (FCNA VOO ≈24.8%; Bradford; NZF UK mutual funds: dividend funds 25%, growth funds full value since one cannot access without selling https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-mutual-funds/).
- Crypto: currency, full market value at 2.5% — NZF UK (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/is-zakat-due-on-bitcoin/ ; https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-cryptocurrencies/), Mufti Faraz Adam (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulfiqh/156648/is-there-a-zakat-levy-on-ones-bitcoin-holdings/), Zakat Foundation (https://www.zakat.org/calculate-zakat-cryptocurrency), Bradford, NZF Canada (NFTs for resale included). Staking rewards: no on-list source; treat as income added at anniversary (inference). Gold ETFs: no on-list source; by analogy gold at full value (Bradford: gold "held as a store of value is considered liable").

## 8. REAL ESTATE
- Residence: exempt by consensus (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/231858/how-to-pay-zakah-on-real-estate ; NZF UK https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/zakat-on-property/). High.
- Rental property: zakat on saved rent only, not value (islamqa.info 231858; NZF UK; Bradford https://joebradford.net/zakat-on-rental-property-income-value-and-the-question-of-timing/ ; Deoband https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/24713/). Minority: Qaradawi 10% of net rent / 5% of gross, by analogy with crops (vol.1 ch.8, printed p.245-247) — optional method, never on building value.
- Land for resale: full market value at anniversary, all schools (islamqa.info 231858; 65515; Taqi Usmani https://muftitaqiusmani.com/en/how-to-pay-zakat/ ; Shafi'i SeekersGuidance Jamir Meah https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/do-i-have-to-pay-zakat-on-land/ ; Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/125095/). Maliki: mudir yearly, muhtakir once on sale (AMJA 79181 https://www.amjaonline.org/fatwa/en/79181/zakat-on-inherited-wealth).
- Unclear intention: Hanafi — intention to trade must accompany purchase; hesitation ("sell or build") = no zakat; later intention effective only on sale (Banuri Town https://www.banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/پلاٹ-کے-خریدتے-وقت-تجارت-کی-حتمی-نیت-نہ-ہو-تو-اس-پر-زکو-ۃ-کا-حکم/27-12-2018 ; Taqi Usmani URL above; Deoband https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/110044/). Majority of all four schools same (Ibn Qudamah via https://islamqa.info/en/answers/249774); a report from Ahmad allows later intention. High.
- Pakistan plot files — the discriminating variable in Pakistani/Deobandi fatwas is whether a SPECIFIC plot is identified and the sale is complete, plus intention; NOT physical possession:
  * Specific plot allotted, bought to resell, no qabza yet → majority: zakat on market value from purchase (Banuri Town 144109201332 https://www.banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/qisto-par-kharede-gay-pilot-main-zakat-ka-hukum-144109201332/06-05-2020 ; 144409100002 on a "plot file" https://banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/sawal-wazeh-nahi-144409100002/23-03-2023 ; Jamia Binoria https://darulifta.info/d/onlinefatawa/fatwa/cssW/ ; Deoband 773/M=7/1436 https://www.darulifta.info/d/deoband/fatwa/dJC/ ; Jamia tur Rasheed mRq citing al-Bahr al-Ra'iq: zakatable from purchase, payment may be deferred to possession then all past years https://darulifta.info/d/jamiaturrasheed/fatwa/mRq/x). Minority: no zakat until qabza (Jamia tur Rasheed 3nv/yc3, citing Ahsan al-Fatawa 4/294 https://www.darulifta.info/d/jamiaturrasheed/fatwa/3nv/ ; Askimam 14969 [snippet]).
  * No plot number / unit non-existent (pure file/booking) → amount paid = receivable (dayn qawi) owed by developer: Jamia Binoria 64280 (https://darulifta.info/d/onlinefatawa/fatwa/csHz/); Banuri Town 144507100972 (non-existent flat: 2.5% on instalments paid https://www.banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/farokht-krne-ki-niyat-se-book-krae-gae-flat-ki-zakat-144507100972/23-01-2024); Leicester (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/7808/); islamqa.info 152090 (bankrupt developer → no zakat until received). Contrary: Deoband 691/D=9/1438 treats it as istisna' — zakat on advances falls on the builder (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/136976/).
  * For own house / undecided → nothing (Binoria 63236; Deoband 111075; Banuri 144111201374).
  * Instalment deduction from a trade plot: Banuri = only instalments due within the year; Jamia tur Rasheed = all remaining; islamqa.info 141551 = none.
  * Mufti Taqi Usmani / Darul Uloom Karachi: no file-specific ruling located; his general rule (firm resale intention at purchase; anniversary-date valuation) applies.
  => The draft's "exempt until possession for all schools" is WRONG as a default: Deobandi/Pakistani majority = zakatable from purchase if bought to resell (possession only affects when payment is due); pure files = receivable; it is only a minority (Jamia tur Rasheed 3nv) that defers to qabza.
- Off-plan/under construction for trade: zakat on current-state value (islamqa.info 231858; 152090 records three views).

## 9. BUSINESS ASSETS
- Valuation: market/selling value at anniversary in all schools (islamqa.info https://islamqa.info/en/answers/65515/ ; Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/5762/ ; Shafi'i Jamir Meah; Hanbali https://islamqa.org/hanbali/hanbalidisciples/154077/). Wholesale vs retail: Ibn Baz/islamqa.info 26236 and islamweb 493495 = by the trader's method of sale; Hanafi Leicester = bulk permissible, "normal wholesale price" preferable (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/daruliftaa/8561/); Islamic Portal = "amount for which something can be sold easily" with discounts factored (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/islamicportal/175666/zakat-on-cost-or-retail-price/); NZF UK = RETAIL price for finished goods (https://nzf.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025_NZF_Business-Zakat-Guide.pdf); Qaradawi p.169 = wholesale; Bradford = cost or wholesale (outlier). → make the basis a selectable setting.
- Receivables (trade & cash) included; NZF excludes service receivables and prepaid expenses (same PDF; https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/debts-owed-to-you/). Raw materials & WIP included at current-state value (Leicester 8561; Mufti Faraz Adam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulfiqh/156637/ ; islamqa.info 125918). Fixed assets, intangibles exempt (islamqa.info 50726; NZF).
- Mid-year inventory: annexed, no separate hawl (Birmingham 77184; islamqa.info 169961; and for goods bought from trade capital islamqa.info 93414).
- Maliki mudir/muhtakir: AMJA 79181 (above); islamweb 156253 on unsold stock (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/156253/).

## 10. RATE & ADVANCE PAYMENT
- 2.5% per lunar year everywhere (islamqa.info 145600; FCNA; AMJA; Bradford; NZF AU).
- 2.577% solar: NZF UK attributes to AAOIFI but "we advise using the lunar calendar" (https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/can-a-solar-year-be-used-for-zakat-calculation/); Bradford gives 2.578% (https://joebradford.net/the-hawl-passing-of-one-lunar-year-for-zakat-liability/); islamweb 157753: Gregorian only under hardship and compensate (https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/157753/); Faraz Rabbani alternative: shift the date back a month every ~2 years. NOT found on zakat.org, zaytuna.edu, fiqhcouncil.org, amjaonline.org, islamqa.info. Qaradawi text: zero hits for 2.577 (a widely repeated attribution is unverified).
- Advance payment: Hanafi — permitted, multiple years, nisab must exist (Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/85041/ ; islamweb 51830 quoting al-Sarakhsi https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/51830/); if nisab absent at year-end the prepayment becomes sadaqah (Tafseer Raheemi https://islamqa.org/hanafi/tafseer-raheemi/179308/). Shafi'i — one year max, four conditions (SeekersGuidance https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/is-advance-payment-of-zakat-permissible-in-the-shafii-school/). Hanbali — up to two years (al-Mardawi via islamweb 51830). Maliki — not permitted except a short period before year-end and makruh (Qaradawi vol.2 p.152: "Rabi'ah, Malik, and Daud do not allow such pre-payment, though some Malikites approve ... no more than one month"; islamweb 51830: "one or two days ... ten ... two months"). islamqa.info: not permitted before owning nisab (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/145090); one or two years ok (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/98528).

## 11. WHO MUST PAY
- Minors/insane: Hanafi — no zakat (except crops & fitr) (Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/127619/ ; Qaradawi vol.1 p.38-39); Maliki/Shafi'i/Hanbali — due, guardian pays from the child's wealth (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/75307/ ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/143840/). High.
- Wife's jewelry: wife is the payer; husband may pay with her permission (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/12338 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/159371/ ; Askimam https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/19171/ ; Deoband https://islamqa.org/hanafi/darulifta-deoband/27693/). High.
- Each adult separate; spouses do not pool to reach nisab (Deoband 147911 [snippet]; SeekersGuidance https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/do-spouses-use-the-same-zakat-date-after-merging-funds/); pooling for payment convenience only when each already exceeds nisab (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/66919/).

## 12. MISSED YEARS (QADA)
- Compute each year on the wealth held at that year's anniversary; estimate if records are missing; remains a debt to Allah/the poor (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/26119/making-up-zakaah-from-past-years ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/99400/ ; SeekersGuidance https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/how-do-i-determine-how-much-zakat-i-owe/).
- Does year-1 unpaid zakat reduce year-2 base? Hanafi YES (unpaid zakat is a debt): SeekersGuidance Weltch 500 g → 12.5 g → next year on 487.5 g (https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/how-do-i-pay-zakat-from-previous-years/); Askimam (https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askimam/103412/); Jamia tur Rasheed 2WF same method for plots. Qaradawi p.70: "past due zakah is considered like debts to other people. It is deductible." islamqa.info (debts don't reduce base) → no deduction by inference. Crude "today's value × 2.5% × years" method (AskMufti https://islamqa.org/hanafi/askmufti/45529/) — not recommended.

## 14. "GENERAL / SAFE MODE" (ihtiyat) vs talfiq
- Talfiq (blameworthy) = mixing two schools within ONE act so the result is valid in neither (SeekersGuidance Hellyer https://seekersguidance.org/articles/featured-articles/clarifying-the-issue-of-talfiq-in-islamic-law-dr-hisham-hellyer/ ; Ilyas Patel https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/what-is-talfiq-and-how-does-one-determine-an-act-when-combining-two-schools/ ; Faraz Rabbani https://seekersguidance.org/answers/hanafi-fiqh/is-it-permissible-to-mix-rulings-between-madhhabs/). Choosing the stricter view per issue is not that; "extricating oneself from the disagreement of the scholars" (al-khuruj min al-khilaf) is recommended (Amjad Rasheed https://seekersguidance.org/answers/shafii-fiqh/ruling-shaving-shortening-beard-shafii-school/); islamqa.info zakat-specific: "it is better to give zakah so as to avoid a matter concerning which the scholars differed" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/128168), silver nisab "most conservative" (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/64). Condemned: tatabbu' al-rukhas (hunting for the easiest view) (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/192787 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/229013).
- Caveats: (1) islamqa.info says choose by evidence, not by strictness; (2) "strictest" is not always well-defined (silver vs gold nisab both have "better for the poor" arguments; Hanafi hawl start/end is stricter for dips but Shafi'i own-hawl is stricter for new money); (3) overpaying is always valid as sadaqah/advance — so "safe mode" = "pay the maximum of the candidate answers" is defensible and should be described as precaution (ihtiyat), not as "the correct ruling". Note NZF's own "pay a bit more to be on the safe side" language (business guide).

## 15. HIJRI CALENDAR
- Hawl runs from the Hijri date nisab was first owned; estimate if unknown; once paid, that date is the anniversary (NZF UK https://nzf.org.uk/knowledge/when-to-pay-zakat ; islamqa.info 207109; Bradford).
- Ramadan: paying in Ramadan is allowed only as ADVANCE payment; delaying past the anniversary to Ramadan is not (https://islamqa.info/en/answers/169899 ; https://islamqa.info/en/answers/8400); many users nonetheless fix Ramadan — NZF and zakat.org encourage pre-paying in Ramadan (https://www.zakat.org/when-do-i-pay-my-zakat-and-how-do-i-calculate-it).
- Umm al-Qura vs sighting: no zakat-specific ruling on any on-list source; islamqa.info says acting on calendars is acceptable for non-Ramadan dates and a one-day error "does not matter" (fasting context, https://islamqa.info/en/answers/180158). A tabular (Umm al-Qura) anniversary with a one-day tolerance is defensible; store the Hijri date, not Gregorian.
- Gregorian fallback: 2.577% (NZF UK/AAOIFI) or 2.578% (Bradford); lunar preferred.

## (b) WHERE THE DRAFT IS WRONG OR OVERSIMPLIFIED
1. Lumps Maliki with Hanbali — they differ on gold/silver combining, hawl nuance for trade goods, annexation of livestock, receivables (once vs all years), advance payment (Maliki forbids), and debt scope. Make four columns.
2. Debt column "Maliki/Hanbali: Conditional — only if no fixed assets (house/car) cover it": wrong framing. Both schools deduct; the condition is offsetting against SURPLUS non-zakatable assets beyond basic needs; a residence and the family car are basic needs and never offset. Also the Hanafi "Full (immediate + 12 months)" is a contemporary fatwa-body rule (NZF, Askimam, SG) with a rival single-instalment camp (Leicester, SG-Hussain, AMJA, zakat.org) and a no-deduction camp (NZF AU, islamqa.info) — not a fixed Hanafi rule; Mufti Taqi Usmani attribution unverified.
3. Shafi'i "None" is correct for the relied-upon view, but islamqa.info (Hanbali-leaning) also says none — the draft's Hanbali "conditional" cell will surprise users of islamqa.info; expose as a choice.
4. Hawl: "Shafi'i continuous, resets if < nisab" is right but the same applies to Maliki and Hanbali; Gemini's "Maliki start/end" is wrong (except trade goods, year-end only). Hanafi "mid-year dips ignored" must add "unless wealth hits zero/below basic needs".
5. Annexation rule missing entirely; Hanbali own-hawl (not with Hanafi); Maliki splits money/livestock.
6. Retirement "balance − 30% for all schools": no body uses 30%; the only 30% is NZF Canada's RRSP withholding tier. Four real positions exist; schools' classical bodies (Deoband, islamqa.info, Jordan) say no zakat until receipt, so "all schools" is false.
7. Stocks "25% for all schools": 25% is NZF UK only; FCNA/Bradford/NZF-CA use 30%; Zaytuna/Zakat Foundation use 100%; AMJA long-term = dividends/zakatable assets. The proxy is an institutional estimate, not a madhhab rule.
8. Plot files "exempt until possession for all": wrong for the Deobandi/Pakistani majority (zakatable from purchase if a specific plot is bought to resell; pure files = receivable); only Jamia tur Rasheed 3nv exempts until qabza. Hanbali/Shafi'i/Maliki have no file-specific rulings.
9. Jewelry Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali "Exempt unless investment": add "or excessive, or haram to wear (men's gold)". Hanafi "100%" correct; note islamqa.info taxes all jewelry.
10. Nisab: draft silent on which grams per body, on gold+silver combining (Shafi'i never; Hanbali disputed), and on cash-with-metal combining.
11. Receivables (owed to user) absent — needed for loans to friends, deposits, unpaid salary, and for plot-file "receivable" cases.
12. Advance payment, minors, spouses, qada deduction, 2.577%, Hijri handling — all absent.
13. "General safe mode = Taxed/Full": "Full debt deduction" is the LENIENT option (reduces zakat); the cautious option is no deduction (Shafi'i). The draft's safe mode mixes strict and lenient choices. (Its "General: start/end" hawl choice, however, IS the max-payment choice and should be kept.)

## (c) MUST BE SURFACED AS "IKHTILAF — CHOOSE" (not silently defaulted)
1. Nisab metal (silver vs gold) and gram standard (85/595 vs 87.48/612.36).
2. Whether gold+silver combine to reach nisab (Shafi'i no; Hanbali disputed).
3. Hawl model: start/end (Hanafi) vs continuous (others); and how a mid-year dip is handled.
4. Annexation of new wealth vs own hawl (with the "pay all at one date as advance" option in non-Hanafi modes).
5. Personal jewelry zakatable or exempt; definition of "excessive" (custom-based; no hard gram threshold).
6. Debt deduction: none / currently-due instalment only / next-12-months / full; and the surplus-asset offset test (Maliki/Hanbali).
7. Student loans and mortgages specifically (NZF UK vs NZF AU vs zakat.org disagree).
8. Receivables: pay yearly vs on receipt; bad debt one year vs all years.
9. Retirement accounts: four positions; tax & penalty rates as user inputs; vesting.
10. Passive stocks: 25% / 30% / company balance-sheet / 100% / dividends-only.
11. Plot files: zakatable from purchase vs from possession; pure file as receivable; instalment deduction scope.
12. Rental property: 2.5% on saved rent vs Qaradawi 10% net rent.
13. Inventory valuation basis: retail / wholesale / method-of-sale / cost.
14. Minors' wealth (Hanafi exempt vs guardian pays).
15. Advance payment allowed (Maliki mode: warn).
16. Lunar 2.5% vs solar 2.577%.
17. Qada: deduct prior unpaid zakat from subsequent bases or not.
18. "Safe mode" semantics: define it as "pay the maximum across selected opinions" and label it precaution (ihtiyat), with islamqa.info's caveat that strictness is not itself a criterion of correctness.
